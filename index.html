BLUEBIRD

Autonomous survey UAV[ Open design ]

BLUEBIRD is a 1.8 m blended-wing drone for conservation and agriculture. An ordinary Android phone is its onboard computer, it plans where it will land before it takes off, and it is built from 3D-printed parts and foam using free tools.

    White paper (PDF)

      Conservation

      Thermal and RGB surveys of wildlife over large, remote areas, with animal detection on board and landing sites chosen ahead of time.

      Agriculture

      Low-cost mapping of fields and pasture, repairable in the field and cheap enough for farms and data-scarce regions.
